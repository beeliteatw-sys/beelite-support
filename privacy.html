BeElite Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

BeElite Athlete Training and Wellness ("BeElite") is built to keep your information on your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored in the app

Workouts, nutrition and hydration logs, sleep and recovery check-ins, injury notes, assignments and grades you enter, goals, and achievements are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled for the app, this data may sync through your own iCloud account, which Apple manages. The developer cannot see it.

Coach Dizzle

Coach Dizzle is an AI training companion. Responses are generated on your device using Apple's on-device model. Your check-in data used for these responses is not sent to the developer.

Permissions

  • Calendar: to read your schedule for reminders and to adjust your wake alarm, and to add reminders you create.
  • Notifications and Alarms: to deliver reminders, check-ins and your wake alarm.
  • Camera: to scan food barcodes. Images are not stored or uploaded by BeElite.

Third-party services

  • Open Food Facts: when you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode or search text is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up nutrition information. Open Food Facts data is available under the Open Database License. If you choose to contribute a missing product, the product details you enter are submitted to Open Food Facts.
  • Google Classroom (optional): if you sign in, BeElite reads your assignments and grades from Google using your authorization. You can revoke access in your Google account at any time.
  • Teamworks (optional): if you sign in with your team's code, BeElite connects to your team's Teamworks service under your team's own terms.
  • Hudl: links open the Hudl app or website. BeElite does not share data with Hudl.

Children

BeElite is intended for student athletes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, because BeElite does not collect personal information on its servers.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. Data synced through iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Health information

BeElite is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
