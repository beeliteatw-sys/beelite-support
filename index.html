Common questions

Do I need an account? No. BeElite works without signing in. Google Classroom and Teamworks sign-in are optional.

Teamworks asks for a team code. Ask your team administrator for your team's code.

Barcode scanning does not work. Allow camera access in Settings > BeElite and use a physical iPhone.

I am not getting reminders or the wake alarm. Allow Notifications, Calendars and Alarms for BeElite in Settings, then check the toggles inside the app's Settings tab.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app to remove data stored on the device. See the privacy policy.

Privacy policy

BeElite is a training and wellness tracking tool and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training or nutrition program.
